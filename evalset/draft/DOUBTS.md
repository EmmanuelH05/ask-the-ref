# Drafter doubts

Things the drafters were unsure about. Read these alongside the review sheet.

## E (false_premise, out_of_scope)

- E-01 (timeouts, gold none): L7.2 and L7.3 mention drinks and cooling breaks. A retriever
  could surface them, and you might want L7.3 as gold.
- E-03 (long-range goal counts double, gold none): the away goals glossary entry says away
  goals "count double". It is about venue, not distance, but it is a strong word-level distractor.
- E-04 (shot clock, gold none): the corpus has countdowns (keeper eight seconds in L12.3,
  throw-in five seconds in L15.2). Not possession limits, but near neighbours.
- E-06 (last man, gold L12.4.9): L12.4.9 never says "last defender"; it settles the premise
  indirectly. L12.4.8 is a plausible alternative gold.
- E-09 (keeper six seconds, gold L12.3): L17.0 also states the eight-second corner kick, so
  two passages show the true rule.
- E-10 (stopping the clock, gold L7.3): G.football-terms.additional-time says much the same,
  and the most direct statement that the referee keeps time is in L5.3.0, which is held out.
- E-14 (league tiebreak by goal difference): nothing covers league tables, but L10.2 and the
  away goals glossary entry cover tiebreaks for one match or a two-leg tie. A deliberate hard negative.
- E-16 (yellow card accumulation): nothing covers suspensions across matches, but L12.4.8
  and L10.3.0 are close.
- E-21 (referee pay): asked about pay only, because appointment of officials is partly
  touched by L6.0 and the glossary.
- Spanish and code-switched drafts use Mexican terms that need your eye: "fuera de lugar",
  "tiempos fuera", "chilena", "cobra el tiro libre", "reloj de posesión", "caminar" for a
  basketball travel.

## A (Laws 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 and the glossary)

- A-09 (L4.1, wedding ring wrapped up): this edition's L4.1 never names jewellery, only
  "accessories" and "dangerous items". The answer is conditional, not a flat yes or no.
- A-12 (away goals paraphrase): avoiding the rule's Spanish words forced "al que más la metió
  de visitante" and "serie a dos juegos", which may sound off. Needs your review most of all.
- A-07 (L1.2, foul on the line of the box): "donde empieza el territorio del portero" is
  stilted because "área", "zona" and "línea" are all in the Spanish passage. The answer is
  also an inference from "lines belong to the areas".
- A-11 (L6.5, VAR paraphrase): "cabina" and "los que están frente a las pantallas" are
  guesses at fan vocabulary. The glossary VMO entry is a near-duplicate (named as "also").
- A-19 (L7.1 + glossary extra time): the 120-minute answer is a sum the reader has to make.
  L10.2 states the same extra-time fact, so L7.1 + L10.2 would also be right. ES uses
  "tiempos extra" where the official term is "tiempo suplementario / prórroga".
- A-16 (L1.14.1) and A-17 (L7.4): each has a second passage that answers alone (L12.4.8 and
  L14.1), named as "also".
- A-06 (outside agent): L3.7 also defines the term but is held out, so the glossary entry is
  the only gold.
- A-18 (L1.10.0 + L7.5): the Spanish edition says "suspender (definitivamente)" for abandon.
  Check that "¿Qué pasa con el partido?" reads right.
- A-20 (L4.1 + L6.2): two-part question. L4.1 alone answers the second half; L6.2 is needed
  only for "which official".
